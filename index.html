Welcome to The Cinder Road
A continuous, single-player fantasy RPG across ten realms inspired by keyboard-driven adventures of the early computer era. You make choices, explore a small world, fight turn-based battles, and roll a virtual twenty-sided die.
Accessibility: Every location has a written description and a list of exits. Use the four labeled travel buttons if you cannot or do not want to use the map. The story and combat outcomes are announced to screen readers. The chronicle can be focused and scrolled with arrow keys. Larger Text and High Contrast are available in the header. No timers, sound cues, or flashing effects.
Explore: Travel with the arrows, WASD, or map. At a special location, use the numbered choices to interact.
Fight: Attacks roll a d20 plus bonuses against enemy defense. Your special class ability uses Focus. Guarding recovers 2 Focus and reduces the next hit. Potions restore HP.
Quest: Speak to Keeper Mara in Briarfall. Your Journal tracks what to do next. Explore the cave and woods for extra encounters and treasure.
World interactions: Buttons beneath the numbered moves list nearby people and items. Each town offers optional branching conversations. Explore deeper caves for puzzle dungeons with no timed inputs. Find keys, unlock optional coffers, return lost tokens for favors, brew healing herbs, and use protective wardstones in battle. All are usable without text commands.
Living world: Speak with Sera the traveling chronicler in each settlement. Look for lost letters in certain forests, study or restore memorials at bridges, and meet an optional roadside trader. These activities have persistent consequences and are never mandatory. They are accessible through both buttons and text commands.
Character growth: Each level above one earns an ability point, including older saves. Spend it on Might (power and health), Agility (accuracy and protection), or Spirit (magic and Focus). These are also used in dungeon skill checks.
Save transfer: Export Save downloads a JSON file you can import on another device. Import replaces the current browser save after confirmation. No server is involved.
Text commands: Optional, off by default. Enable TEXT COMMANDS in the header, then type commands such as “go left”, “talk to Mara”, “pick up brick”, “look”, “use potion”, “attack”, “journal”, and “help”. Buttons and keyboard shortcuts continue to work.
Progress: The game autosaves in this browser. Defeat sends you back to town with a gold penalty, not a permanent game over. Your adventure continues automatically from one region to the next after you defeat its central foe. The journey leads to a final confrontation and two endings. Your character and journey progress save locally.
