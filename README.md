# The Cinder Road

**A free, old-school fantasy text adventure.**  
*One traveler. Ten realms. A bell that should never have fallen silent.*

The Cinder Road is a browser-based, single-player role-playing game inspired by the spirit of classic BBS adventures, early computer RPGs, and tabletop fantasy campaigns. Follow a continuous story, explore forgotten places, roll the dice in battle, meet people who remember your choices, and decide what kind of legend you leave behind.

## [▶ Play The Cinder Road](https://authorpa.github.io/the-cinder-road/)

**No download, account, or installation required.** Open the link in a modern desktop or mobile browser and begin your adventure.

Want to play without a connection? Download this repository's `index.html` file and open it in your browser. Your offline save and website save may be stored separately.

## Your adventure awaits

- **Create your adventurer:** Choose a Knight, Ranger, or Mystic.
- **Explore the world:** Travel across an old-school map through settlements, forests, ruins, and hidden locations.
- **Follow one continuous campaign:** Journey across ten connected regions, with no artificial chapter-ending interruptions.
- **Battle with dice:** Face wandering foes and powerful guardians in turn-based, d20-inspired combat.
- **Find your own path:** Explore optional dungeons, solve environmental puzzles, and collect useful items.
- **Make decisions that matter:** Help people, uncover lost histories, and encounter characters who remember what you've done.
- **Grow stronger:** Gain experience, improve your Might, Agility, and Spirit, and upgrade your equipment.

You can follow the main quest or take time to investigate the roads less traveled. Optional adventures are **not required** to finish the story.

## Getting started

1. Select **Play The Cinder Road** above.
2. Watch or skip the optional retro ASCII introduction.
3. Name your adventurer and choose a class.
4. Read the story, choose an action, and begin exploring.

**New to RPGs?** There's a **HOW TO PLAY** button inside the game, and all combat is turn-based: take your time.

## Controls

| Action | Controls |
| --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys, **W A S D**, on-screen direction buttons, or an adjacent map tile |
| Choose an action | Click/tap its button, or press the matching number **1–9** |
| Open your pack | **I**, or select the **PACK** tab |
| Open your journal | **J**, or select the **JOURNAL** tab |
| Navigate interface controls | **Tab**, **Shift + Tab**, **Enter**, and **Space** |
| Enter a written command | Turn on **TEXT COMMANDS**, type a command, and submit it |

**Text commands are optional and off by default.** If you want that classic text-adventure experience, switch them on and try:

```text
look
go north
talk to innkeeper
pick up brick
inventory
use potion
attack
```

Text commands support common phrases, not unlimited free-form conversation. Every required action also has a button-based alternative.

## Accessibility and play options

The Cinder Road is designed to be usable without relying on the visual map or fast reactions:

- Keyboard-accessible game controls and written travel directions
- Screen-reader labels and text-based story information
- **LARGER TEXT** and **HIGH CONTRAST** options
- Reduced-motion support, plus controls to skip or read the introduction at your own pace
- **COMBAT: STORY** for gentler battles and fewer random encounters; **COMBAT: CLASSIC** is the default
- No timed combat decisions

Accessibility is an ongoing priority. The game has received keyboard and automated testing, but has **not** yet completed a comprehensive audit with screen-reader users. Please report issues so they can be fixed.

## Saving your adventure

The game automatically saves progress in your browser when local storage is available.

- Use **EXPORT SAVE** to download a backup of your character.
- Use **IMPORT SAVE** to restore a backup or move your adventure to another browser or device.
- **RESTORE BACKUP** may recover a previous local save if the browser has one available.
- Clearing browser storage, changing browsers, or moving between the hosted website and a downloaded HTML copy can result in separate saved games.

**Recommendation:** Export your save occasionally, especially before changing devices or clearing browser data.

## A few common questions

**Is the game free?**  
Yes. It requires no purchase or subscription.

**Is this multiplayer?**  
Not yet. The current game is a complete **single-player** adventure. An optional online shared-world mode is a future idea, not a feature of this release.

**Do I need to know D&D or old BBS games?**  
No. The game explains your available actions as you play.

**Can I play on a phone?**  
Yes. The interface includes touch controls and adapts to smaller screens.

## Feedback and bug reports

Found a bug, a confusing command, a difficult-to-read screen, or an accessibility barrier? You can [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/authorpa/the-cinder-road/issues). Include what happened, what you expected, and (if relevant) your browser and device. Please don't post exported save files publicly if you consider your character data private.
